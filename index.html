Profile

From youth leadership to AI cinema.

Muawia Ismail Ibrahim is a Sudanese youth leader, filmmaker and AI prompt engineering specialist.

He previously served as Director of the Office of the Federal Minister of Youth and Sports and participated in national and international youth activities.

His interests include artificial intelligence, prompt engineering, filmmaking, digital creativity, youth empowerment and the preservation of Sudanese and Nile Valley heritage through modern storytelling.

He is also Secretary-General of the Sudanese Youth Empowerment Authority and works to promote opportunities for young people in technology, innovation, media and entrepreneurship.
