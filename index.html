Professional Profile

From youth leadership to AI cinema.

Muawia Ismail Ibrahim is a Sudanese youth leader, filmmaker and AI prompt engineering specialist.

He serves as Secretary-General of the Sudanese Youth Empowerment Authority and has worked in youth and public-sector initiatives.

He previously served as Director of the Office of the Federal Minister of Youth and Sports.

His filmmaking work focuses on the use of generative AI to develop historical, humanitarian and animated stories.

His film DJEDI explores Ancient Egyptian history through a modern AI-assisted production workflow, while THE GATE OF BARKAL explores the history of Kush and King Taharqa.
