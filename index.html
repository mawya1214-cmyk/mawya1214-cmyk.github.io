Professional Profile

From youth leadership to AI cinema.

Muawia Ismail Ibrahim is a Sudanese filmmaker, AI prompt engineering specialist and youth leader. He is also the Secretary-General of the Sudanese Youth Empowerment Authority and a member of the Asia Digital Art and Design Association.

He previously served as Director of the Office of the Federal Minister of Youth and Sports and has participated in youth and public-sector activities connecting Sudanese youth with regional and international initiatives.

In 2024, he participated in Youth Connect Africa in Rwanda as part of a Sudanese government delegation led by the then-Minister of Youth and Sports. He was also involved in youth activities and public events in Sudan, including activities in Al-Ti and Al-Suraiha in Gezira State.

AI Credential1 Million Prompters — AI prompt-engineering training under the initiative of Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, certified by Dubai Future Foundation.
